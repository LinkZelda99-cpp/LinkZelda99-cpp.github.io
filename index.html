About Me

I'm Austin McDonald, a programmer and game developer who loves turning ideas into things people can actually experience.

My work spans game development, software projects, and the systems that bring them together. I'm currently the lead programmer and designer of SOLUNE, while also developing Echoes of Shadows, a C++ RPG that lets me explore software architecture and game systems firsthand.

I enjoy going deep into the things I build, whether that means solving a difficult programming problem, experimenting with a new technology, or creating something simply because I'm curious about how it could work.

This website is part of that process: a place to showcase my work, document my growth, and keep building something better as I learn.
